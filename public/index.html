About

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU). My work sits where human–computer interaction meets speech and language technology: I care about how AI systems fit into the way people actually talk, read, and decide.

I also enjoy the lower layers — networking, containers, and the infrastructure that keeps real-time systems running.

Selected work

  • OmniObserve

    Senior research project, HAIX Lab, NYCU, advised by Prof. Grace Yu-Chun Yen

    An online meeting facilitator that helps hidden consensus surface. Participants note private thoughts by quick voice input; the system transcribes them, turns them into idea blocks with an LLM, finds similar ideas across people with embeddings, and sends anonymous cues so unspoken agreement can reach the group discussion.

    My part The speech pipeline (Breeze ASR, voice activity detection, streaming transcription), the private board in React (idea block editing, manual input, linking transcripts to ideas), and study task design.

    React, TypeScript, FastAPI, WebSocket, Breeze ASR, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Jitsi
    GitHubDemo video

  • SoulSee: an AI photo booth that keeps you in charge

    OpenHCI 2026, team of five. Best Application Award and Best Popularity Award; demoed at TAICHI 2026

    AI proposes a reference shot from what you describe, then live pose detection and the phone's orientation sensor guide you one hint at a time. You decide whether to match it, drift from it, or shoot your own version. Camera data never leaves the browser.

    My part Problem definition and ideation, usability testing during development, refining the concept after judges' feedback, and presenting the system to visitors at the final showcase and TAICHI.

    JavaScript, MediaPipe Pose, Gemini and OpenAI image models, Cloudflare Workers
    GitHubLive demo

  • 3D fireworks over a lake

    Computer Graphics course, team of two

    A real-time OpenGL scene extending an open-source fireworks renderer with a wave-animated lake that reflects the fireworks, procedural trees and mountains, explosion-driven dynamic lighting, and HDR bloom.

    My part The lake: wave animation, Fresnel shading and firework reflections, plus building the surrounding scene.

    C++, OpenGL, GLSL
    GitHubDemo video

  • Evaluating LLM-generated recipes

    Artificial Intelligence course, team of four

    ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude generated recipes from photos of Taiwanese dishes. We compared text metrics with our own importance-weighted Recipe-F1 and a user study: BERTScore matched human ratings most often per recipe, while Recipe-F1 came closest on average for cooking steps.

    My part BERT-based evaluation, fried-chicken data collection and evaluation, and collecting ChatGPT's responses.

    Python, BERTScore, ROUGE, bert-base-chinese
    GitHubSlides
