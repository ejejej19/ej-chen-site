About

I’m a fourth-year Computer Science student at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU). My work sits where human–computer interaction meets speech and language technology: I care about how AI systems fit into the way people actually talk, read, and decide.

I also enjoy the lower layers — networking, containers, and the infrastructure that keeps real-time systems running.

Selected work

  • Real-time meeting transcription

    Senior project, HAIX Lab, NYCU

    An AI-assisted meeting transcription system with live speech recognition. I work on the audio pipeline and streaming architecture, and explore Mandarin ASR models for real-time captioning over WebSocket.

    Speech recognition, WebSocket streaming, audio processing

  • Multimodal retrieval for RAG

    Natural Language Processing course

    A hybrid retrieval pipeline combining BM25 and BGE-M3 dense retrieval with reciprocal rank fusion, cross-encoder reranking, and a learning-to-rank stage (XGBRanker) on engineered features.

    Python, BGE-M3, cross-encoders, XGBoost

  • Containerized 5G core network

    Networking lab

    Built and debugged an end-to-end 5G stack with srsRAN and Open5GS running in Docker on Ubuntu.

    srsRAN, Open5GS, Docker

  • SDN with live monitoring

    Networking lab

    An OpenFlow network on Open vSwitch controlled by ONOS, with a Prometheus and Grafana dashboard for traffic monitoring.

    OpenFlow, OVS, ONOS, Prometheus, Grafana
